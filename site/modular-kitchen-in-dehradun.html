Before you buy

What to look for

Pick the layout

Straight, L-shape, U-shape, parallel or island. Your room shape and where the window and plumbing sit usually decide it.

Choose the material

Steel resists termites, oil and moisture best. BWP ply gives a warm wooden look. HDHMR offers the widest shutter finishes.

Measure carefully

Note wall lengths, window and door positions, and where the sink and gas point are. A photo helps too.
