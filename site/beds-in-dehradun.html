Before you buy

What to look for

King or queen

A king bed takes a 72 x 78 inch mattress and a queen a 60 x 78 inch one. Leave about 60 cm on the sides you walk around.

Choose your storage

Hydraulic storage lifts the whole base for easy access. Box storage with front drawers suits rooms where the bed sits against two walls.

Buy the mattress together

Matching the mattress to the bed frame avoids gaps and sagging. We can help you pick both in the showroom.
