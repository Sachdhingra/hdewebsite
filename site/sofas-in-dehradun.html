Before you buy

What to look for

Measure the wall first

Leave about 75 cm in front for walking and a centre table. An L-shape suits a corner; a 3+1+1 set suits a square room.

Pick fabric for your home

Leatherette wipes clean and suits homes with children. Fabric breathes better through Dehradun summers.

Sit before you choose

Seat depth and cushion firmness feel very different in person. Come and try a few in the showroom.
