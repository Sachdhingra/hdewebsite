Before you buy

What to look for

Check the height

Measure from floor to ceiling, and leave room above for the top to clear any beam or fan.

Locker and mirror

Choose an almirah with an inside locker for valuables and a mirror if you will dress in the same room.

Door space

Hinged doors need space in front to open fully. Sliding wardrobes suit narrow rooms.
