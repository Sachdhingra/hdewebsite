Before you buy

What to look for

Pick the lock type

Manual lockers need no batteries. Digital and biometric lockers open without a key, so there is nothing to lose.

Size for what you keep

Measure jewellery boxes and document files before you choose an inside size.

Plan where it goes

Lockers are heavy, so choose the spot before delivery and keep it out of sight.
