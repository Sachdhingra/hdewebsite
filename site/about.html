Our story

Furnishing Dehradun homes since 2006.

Authorised channel partner of

Home Decor Enterprises began in 2006 with a simple idea. Families in Dehradun should be able to buy furniture that lasts from people they can walk back to.

We became a Godrej Interio channel partner that year. It gave us a range we could stand behind, and we wanted our customers to have that same confidence: a sofa that keeps its shape, a wardrobe whose doors still close properly years later, and a warranty that means something because the brand behind it is still around.

The rest we had to learn one home at a time. Rooms are rarely standard sizes, so we started measuring before we suggested anything. Customers told us they disliked surprises on the bill, so we began quoting everything up front. People wanted to know who would turn up at their door, so our own team does the delivery and fitting.

Twenty years on we run two showrooms, one in Karanpur near Parade Ground and one on Saharanpur Road in Patel Nagar. Our range has grown to cover whole homes, from sofas, beds and mattresses to modular kitchens and wardrobes. Many of the people who walk in today were sent by someone we furnished a home for.

We are still a local business, and we like it that way. If something you bought from us needs attention, you call the showroom and someone you have met picks up. We are grateful for the trust Dehradun has shown us, and we try to earn it again with every order.
