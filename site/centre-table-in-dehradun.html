Before you buy

What to look for

Match the sofa height

A centre table a little lower than the sofa seat is easiest to reach from where you sit.

Leave walking space

Keep about 45 cm between the table and the sofa so people can pass and stretch their legs.

Pick a top for your home

Solid wood and engineered wood suit everyday use; glass and marble look lighter but need coasters and care.
