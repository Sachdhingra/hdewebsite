Before you buy

What to look for

Allow room for chairs

Leave about 90 cm between the table edge and the wall so chairs can be pulled out.

Seats per person

Allow about 60 cm of table edge for each person. A 6-seater suits most families of four with guests.

Material

Solid sheesham wood is sturdy and warm. Engineered wood and glass tops are lighter and easy to clean.
