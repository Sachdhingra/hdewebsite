Before you buy

Common questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Talk to us in either showroom or over the phone, free and with no obligation to order.

Do you charge for delivery?

Delivery and installation are free. If you live outside Dehradun, call us and we will tell you what is possible for your area.

Do you offer EMI or exchange?

Yes. No-cost EMI is available on card and paper finance, and you can exchange your old furniture with free pickup. We accept cash, cards and online payment.

How long does a modular kitchen take?

Most kitchens are installed a few weeks after the design is approved. We give you a date with your quote.

Can I get a wardrobe, bed or sofa customised?

Yes. Many wardrobes, beds and sofas can be customised for size, finish and fabric. Bring your room measurements to the showroom or share them over the phone.

Is there parking at the showrooms?

Yes, both showrooms have free parking on site.

Who handles warranty and service?

We do. Call the showroom you bought from and we arrange the service visit.
