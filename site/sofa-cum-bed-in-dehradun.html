Before you buy

What to look for

Measure it open

Check the room fits the bed when it is fully open, with space to walk around it, not just the sofa.

Try the mechanism

Open and close it in the showroom. It should be easy enough for whoever will do it most often.

Sleep or sit

If guests stay often, look at the mattress thickness. If it is mostly a sofa, choose for seat comfort.
