Before you buy

What to look for

Measure your bed

Check the inside length and width of your bed frame. Common sizes are 72 x 36 (single), 78 x 60 (queen) and 78 x 72 inches (king).

Firm or soft

People with back pain often prefer a firmer, orthopaedic mattress. Side sleepers usually like a softer top.

Look at the warranty

Godrej Interio mattresses carry the warranty shown on each product. Keep your bill safe.
