Why you will like it

NX Advanced offers 3-in-1 access with digital, biometric, and key lock options. It delivers advanced security with an inbuilt I-Buzz alarm for added protection, while premium interiors ensure a seamless blend of convenience, style, and reliable security for your valuables.

Dual Mode Locking (Digital + Biometric with Key Lock)

Equipped with fingerprint and digital keypad access, this locker operates in combination with a key-operated base lock for enhanced security. It supports up to 50 fingerprints, 10 individual 6-digit user codes, and 1 master code for controlled access.

3-Way Live Shooting Bolt

Equipped with an advanced 3-Way Live Shooting Bolt system, this powerful mechanism features 7Front + 2Top + 2Bottom live bolts, ensuring comprehensive security. Additionally, the safe is reinforced with 4 Rear fixed bolts, adding an extra layer of defense against unauthorized access, keeping your most precious belongings safe and secure.

Enhanced Security with inbuilt i-Buzz Alarm & Door Open Alerts

If someone tries to tamper or force open the locker, the i-Buzz alarm triggers, alerting you to potential unauthorized access. Plus, if the door stays open too long, an audible alert reminds you to close it, adding extra protection for your valuables

Voice Based Acknowledgements

Clear voice-based prompts guide users through each step, ensuring smooth operation and minimizing user errors. This intuitive system enhances convenience and makes access simpler for every user.

Emergency Mechanical Override and External USB Charging Support

In the event of a low-battery condition, the lock offers an emergency mechanical override key to ensure uninterrupted access. Additionally, the external USB charging provision enables immediate power restoration, maintaining seamless and reliable operation at all times.

Autolock Keypad Freeze Function

Automatic sensor freeze function that keeps your valuables safe by preventing unauthorised access after 3 incorrect entries for numeric codes and 5 incorrect finger scan entries.
