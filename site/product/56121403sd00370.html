Why you will like it

Eira blends natural travertine with warm mango wood to create a composed centrepiece. Rooted in history yet suited to modern living, it brings quiet character and enduring presence to the room.

Timeless travertine stone top

The Eira Coffee Table is crafted from natural travertine stone, celebrated since ancient Roman architecture for its strength and visual depth. The table’s subtle pores and organic veining lend it a distinctive, enduring character.

Generously rounded tabletop for everyday ease

The spacious, softly rounded surface of this coffee table offers ample room for daily essentials and decorative accents. The curved profile enhances movement around the table while maintaining a welcoming, balanced form in living spaces.

Robust mango wood base for steady support

The understructure of this coffee table is built from solid mango wood, known for its density and natural resilience. This well-crafted foundation ensures reliable stability, keeping the table steady and confident during everyday use.

Warm wood and cool stone contrast

By pairing earthy mango wood with cool travertine stone, this coffee table creates a refined visual dialogue. This contrast adds depth, elegance, and a composed sense of calm to both modern and transitional interiors.
