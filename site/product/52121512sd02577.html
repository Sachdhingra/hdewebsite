Why you will like it

The Bauhaus collection was born out of a desire to break the monotony and bring a sense of play into everyday spaces. More than just cushions, they are vibrant statements designed to awaken any space with a burst of energy.

High-quality prints for lasting colour and vibrancy

A great print is only as good as how long it stays looking that way, which is why this cover is digitally printed using high-quality disperse inks. The result is excellent colour retention and colour fastness, helping prevent fading and keeping the Bauhaus design vibrant through everyday use.

Sturdy zipper closure for lasting durability

The small components of a cushion cover are often what determine how long it lasts, which is why the Bauhaus cover is finished with a high-quality zipper designed for smooth, repeated use. It opens and closes with ease, ensuring durability and extending the overall life of the product.

Shrink-resistant fabric for a fine, lasting finish

Fabric that holds its shape over time makes all the difference to how a cushion looks on the sofa or bed. The cotton slub material used in this cover is shrink-resistant, ensuring the cover keeps its fit and fine finish through everyday use and repeated washing.
