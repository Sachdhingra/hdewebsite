Why you will like it

Step-In XL maximises wall-mounted shoe storage with capacity for twelve pairs. Designed with synchronised doors and central locking, it transforms entryways into organised, space-efficient zones.

Twelve-pair storage capacity

With four levels and three-pair capacity per level, this shoe cabinet accommodate twelve pairs of shoes comfortably. This generous sizing suits medium to large households or shoe enthusiasts, providing comprehensive storage without requiring multiple cabinets.

Linked door mechanism for efficiency

All four compartment doors connect together, opening and closing simultaneously with a single pull. This synchronised operation eliminates repetitive door handling, making shoe access remarkably quick during rushed mornings or when guests arrive.

Central locking security

One lock secures all twelve-pair capacity simultaneously, protecting your entire footwear collection. This unified locking feature provides comprehensive security in shared living situations, keeping shoes safely stored with minimal key management.

Wall-mounted space optimisation

Crafted from high quality CRCA steel, the Step-in mounts directly to your wall using tear-drop holes, freeing up valuable floor space entirely. This suspended installation keeps entryways unobstructed and walkable, making even narrow hallways feel spacious and welcoming.

Integrated ventilation system

Strategic ventilation openings promote continuous air circulation through each compartment. This airflow prevents moisture accumulation and odour development, keeping stored shoes fresher and extending their lifespan by reducing dampness-related deterioration.

Anti-rust powder coating with integrated ventilation

Premium epoxy powder coating provides superior rust protection and a smooth, durable finish with strategic ventilation openings that promote continuous air circulation through each compartment. This airflow prevents moisture accumulation and odour development, keeping stored shoes fresher while advanced coating withstands humidity and daily contact, without demanding special maintenance.
