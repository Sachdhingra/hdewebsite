Why you will like it

Your security needs are evolving — and so should your locker. The NX Pro Slide combines 10X stronger construction with cutting-edge dual mode access and advanced safety features. Its sleek drawer-style design, motorised lock and voice acknowledgements make safeguarding your valuables effortless.

Dual Mode Locking (Digital + Biometric)

This lock features advanced dual-access functionality with fingerprint recognition and a digital keypad, supporting up to 50 fingerprints, 10 individual 6-digit user codes, and 1 master code for secure, controlled access.

Ultimate Security with Advanced Motorised shooting Bolts

Powered by an internal motor, these bolts remain firmly locked until the correct Password is authorised, ensuring the safe cannot be opened forcefully through magnet manipulation or vigorous shaking.

Voice Based Acknowledgements

Clear voice-based prompts guide users through each step, ensuring smooth operation and minimizing user errors. This intuitive system enhances convenience and makes access simpler for every user.

Autolock Keypad Freeze Function

Automatic sensor freeze function that keeps your valuables safe by preventing unauthorised access after 4 consecutive wrong fingerprint entries

Emergency Mechanical Override and External USB Charging Support

In the event of a low-battery condition, the lock offers an emergency mechanical override key to ensure uninterrupted access. Additionally, the external USB charging provision enables immediate power restoration, maintaining seamless and reliable operation at all times.

Built-in Buzz Alarm: Enhanced Security for Your Home Locker

Our locker features a built-in buzz alarm that adds an extra layer of protection for your documents. If anyone attempts to tamper with or force open the locker, the alarm will activate, instantly alerting you to potential unauthorized access.
