Why you will like it

Chubby is your cosy corner of calm—high-backed, cloud-soft, and wrapped in rich upholstery with sleek leather accents. The chaise invites you to stretch out, lounge, and stay a little longer.

High-back ease with built-in support

The tall backrest is shaped to follow the natural curve of your spine, giving your upper back and shoulders a relaxed, supported feel. Under the softness, layered foam and lumbar-friendly profiling keep you comfortable through long conversations, reading sessions, or weekend naps.

Fluffy, nest-like seating

Generous foam cushioning creates that “fall-into-it” plushness while still holding its shape. The slightly voluminous proportions make the seat feel like a personal nook, making Chubby ideal when you want to curl up with a book or simply unwind at the end of the day.

Rounded armrests for gentle support

Smooth, rounded armrests are designed at just the right height to catch your elbows and forearms without digging in. Their soft curves mirror the back and seat, giving your body continuous support and reinforcing the sofa’s welcoming, cloud-like feel.

Soft curves, lifted silhouette

Chubby’s flowing lines and elevated legs combine to keep the design visually light while still feeling substantial. The ground clearance not only makes cleaning easier but also lets light travel underneath, so the sofa never feels heavy in compact rooms.

Generous ground clearance

Soft bouclé fabric adds warmth, depth, and a rich tactile feel to the seating, while the leatherette accents along the sides lend a clean, structured finish. This thoughtful contrast of textures creates a sophisticated, layered look that balances everyday durability with elevated style.

Stretch-out comfort with L-shaped chaise

The extended chaise section lets you put your feet up, change posture through the day, or share the sofa more comfortably, transforming Chubby from a single-seat retreat into a full-body lounging zone.
