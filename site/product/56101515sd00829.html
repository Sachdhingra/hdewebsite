Why you will like it

ErgoNest is built on a simple but powerful idea — two people sharing a bed shouldn't have to compromise on comfort. With an incline control and intelligent storage, it redefines modern bedrooms.

Individual mattress incline control for personalised rest

Each side of the bed adjusts independently between 3 and 60 degrees using a dedicated RF remote, letting one person read upright while the other sleeps flat. Beyond convenience, the incline feature actively supports back pain relief, reduces acid reflux, and helps ease sleep apnea symptoms including snoring.

Motorised lift-up storage for effortless storage access

A single press of the button on the headboard ledge engages the motor and raises the entire mattress base, opening up approximately 845 litres of storage beneath. The mechanism only operates on a continuous press, stopping immediately when released — a deliberate safety feature that prevents accidental closure.

Built-in charging on both sides for a cable-free bedside

The headboard integrates a 3-pin socket, a USB-C port, and a USB-A port on each side, so both users can charge phones, tablets, or laptops without cables draping across the bed. Everything charges where it sits, invisibly.

Raised platform for a floating, elevated look

The 4-inch measured floor clearance creates a light, suspended appearance that adds visual depth to the bed frame. It also keeps the space beneath open and accessible, making cleaning and maintenance straightforward.
