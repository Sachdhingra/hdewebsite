Why you will like it

Great cushions get noticed for how they feel as much as how they look. The Velvet Dual Tone Cushion Cover is made from soft, quality velvet and has contrast accent piping for texture and detail.

Quality velvet for a soft, smooth touch

Some cushions don’t just look great but feel great to touch too. The Velvet Dual Tone Cushion Cover is made from quality solid-hued velvet, with a soft, even surface that feels pleasant under your fingers and against your back. The fabric makes a cushion that’s as comfortable to lean on as it is to look at.

Contrast accent piping for a neat, defined edge

The piping on this cushion cover is a small detail that does a lot. A contrast accent runs around the seams, tracing the shape of the cushion and giving the cover its dual-tone character. This thoughtful detailing adds visual interest to a solid velvet surface, and makes the cushion look neatly tailored from every angle.

Smooth zipper for quick, repeated cover changes

Changing the cushion covers should never feel like a hassle. Made with a high-quality zipper, this cushion cover is easy to take off and put on. Also, the zipper is made for repeated use, which helps the cover stay in good order for longer.

Shrink-resistant fabric that holds its fit

The fabric of the Velvet Dual Tone Cushion Cover is shrink-resistant, so it keeps its size and continues to fit a cushion well. It also ensures that the cushion maintains a put-together finish and the piping stays well-defined.
