Why you will like it

Lumi coffee table pairs the cool elegance of natural marble with the warmth of sculpted mango wood. Its rounded form and generous surface create a refined focal point for modern living spaces.

Spacious tabletop for effortless hosting

The smooth, white natural marble surface of Lumi offers ample room for books, beverages, and decorative accents. Its generous proportions support both everyday use and relaxed gatherings.

Rounded design with natural marble surface

The circular marble top introduces softness and balance into the room. The table’s polished finish adds a refined, luminous quality that enhances contemporary interiors.

Crafted from mango wood, the understructure provides dependable strength and durability. The table’s solid construction ensures long-term stability without compromising design elegance.
