Why you will like it

The Velvet Leaf Cushion Cover is made from embossed velvet with a delicate, repeating leaf pattern. It adds a quiet detail to a couch or a bed, and it’s easy to style with whatever is already there.

Embossed velvet for a surface you can see and feel

The Velvet Leaf Cushion Cover is made from quality velvet that has been embossed, so the design is pressed into the pile and gives the surface a raised, tactile quality. The velvet feels soft to the hand, and the embossing catches the light and adds depth to the cover.

Dainty repeating leaf pattern for an easy, go-to staple

Some cushions work in nearly any setting, and this is one of them. The Velvet Leaf Cushion Cover carries a fine, repeating leaf pattern that’s delicate enough to stay understated. This cushion cover can make your cushion easily complement the sofa decor or the bedroom décor; it can also be paired with plain cushions or embroidered ones, showcasing its versatility.

High-quality zipper that moves smoothly every time

Taking a cover off shouldn’t take effort. The Velvet Leaf Cushion Cover is fitted with a high-quality zipper that glides open and closed, so changing or cleaning the cover is quick. It’s built for repeated use, which helps the cover last longer and stay in good shape.

Shrink-resistant fabric for a lasting, snug fit

The Velvet Leaf Cushion Cover is made from shrink-resistant fabric, so it keeps its shape and ensures the cushion looks as plump as it looked on day one. The fabric also ensures that the embossed pattern stays crisp, and the finish stays fine.
