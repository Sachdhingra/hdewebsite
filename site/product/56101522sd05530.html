Why you will like it

The Tecno Gaming Chair is designed as an all-around command seat for gaming, study, and work-from-home setups. It combines immersive comfort with precise support, so you can focus longer with less fatigue.

Breathable knitted comfort

The main surfaces of Techno use soft knitted fabric that feels gentle against the skin while allowing heat and moisture to dissipate. Mesh panels and synthetic leather at the rear side of the frame add extra airflow while adding a classic touch to the seating.

Recline and relax

A smooth reclining mechanism lets you lean back from an upright 90 degrees to a relaxed 108 degrees, helping you shift posture, ease back tension, and find your personal comfort zone between focus and downtime.

Customisable lumbar support

An adjustable lumbar element lets you fine-tune lower-back support, so the chair adapts to your spine instead of the other way around. This targeted reinforcement helps reduce slouching and discomfort during extended use.

Soft curves with an elevated presence

A smooth gas lift allows easy seat-height changes within a 411–510 mm range, so you can align your eyes with the screen and your arms with the desk at the right ergonomic level.

Bucket seat for locked-in comfort

The bucket-style seat cradles your hips and thighs, keeping you centred even during intense gameplay while still allowing natural movement. Its contouring helps distribute pressure more evenly across the seat, reducing numbness and encouraging better posture over time.

High backrest that envelops you

A tall backrest supports the full length of your spine and shoulders, creating a cocooned feel that enhances immersion. The generous height also pairs well with head and neck support, so leaning back between rounds feels restful rather than straining.
