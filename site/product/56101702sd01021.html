Why you will like it

Designed for organised workspaces, the Storo Quick Cabinet combines secure storage, smooth-glide drawers, and robust steel construction to support efficient, clutter-free document management.

Advanced anti-tip safety mechanism

The Storo Quick cabinet is equipped with an intelligent anti-tip system that allows only one drawer to open at a time. This safety feature enhances stability, reducing the risk of toppling even when drawers are fully loaded.

Smooth-glide drawers for effortless access

Precision telescopic slides of the cabinet enable each drawer to open and close smoothly with minimal effort. Ergonomic aluminium alloy handles provide a comfortable grip, while the anti-rebound mechanism keeps drawers securely in place when closed.

Centralised locking system for secure storage

A built-in cam lock allows all drawers to be secured through a single locking point. This practical feature helps protect important files and documents, offering added peace of mind in shared work environments.

Organised label holder design

Integrated aluminium alloy label holders make categorising and locating files simple. This filing cabinet supports quick identification and retrieval of documents, helping maintain an efficient workflow.

Robust CRCA steel construction

Built using a durable CRCA steel frame, the filing cabinet is engineered for long-term reliability. Its sturdy structure withstands the demands of everyday office use while maintaining a clean, professional appearance.
