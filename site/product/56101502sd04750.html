Why you will like it

Adore reimagines corner seating with its soft, enveloping design. Featuring plush, upholstered seating, it transforms living rooms into inviting retreats for family gatherings and relaxed evenings.

Corner-optimised l-shape configuration

Designed in L-shape with a chaise, Adore fits seamlessly into corner spaces while maximising seating capacity. This configuration creates natural conversation zones and makes efficient use of room corners that often remain underutilised.

Soft sinking comfort for relaxation

Plush slab stock foam across the seat, back, and armrests creates a gentle, cloud-like feel that lets you sink into relaxation. Additionally, the sofa’s soft polyester fabric offers a smooth, cool-to-touch surface that feels gentle against your skin.

Mid-back enveloping design

Mid-height backrests cradle your entire torso from lower back to shoulders, providing supportive comfort that wraps around your body. This enveloping structure encourages deep settling, making extended lounging sessions genuinely comfortable and restorative.

Broad angular armrests for versatility

Wide, angled armrests accommodate various relaxing postures—whether you're leaning sideways with a book, propping up a tablet, or simply resting your arms. This versatile design adapts to how you naturally want to sit and unwind throughout the day.

Solid wood framework

Durable solid wood construction with quality plywood reinforcement ensures the L-shaped sofa remains sturdy through years of family use.

Elevated wooden legs for floor clearance

Solid wood legs lift the sofa off the ground, creating accessible space underneath for hassle-free sweeping and vacuuming. This practical elevation keeps your living area clean without requiring furniture rearrangement during routine maintenance.
