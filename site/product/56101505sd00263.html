Why you will like it

Cinq 1500 Console is designed to create moments of pause within everyday spaces. With its layered storage and understated detailing, it brings quiet sophistication to entryways, hallways, and bedrooms.

Minimal modern styling for versatile placement

The console’s restrained silhouette and balanced proportions allow it to settle effortlessly into varied spaces, without overpowering the surroundings. Its soft, contemporary neutral palette creates a composed backdrop that pairs seamlessly with both muted interiors and statement decor elements.

Tubular steel structure for light architectural character

The slender tubular steel frame introduces a refined linear aesthetic while providing dependable support and long-term durability.

Integrated storage for everyday essentials

Two drawers discreetly organise keys, accessories, documents, and daily-use belongings, helping maintain a neat and uncluttered environment. Additionally, the open storage section allows books, decor accents, and collectibles to be displayed with ease, adding personality and warmth to the space.

Elevated profile for airy spatial balance

Raised from the ground, the console maintains visual openness and allows easy cleaning beneath, making interiors feel lighter and more spacious.
