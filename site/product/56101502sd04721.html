Why you will like it

With soft upholstery and a lounge-ready seat, Denite glides into a full sleeping surface in just two simple moves. Adjustable cushions and a sleep-friendly mattress make it a one-of-a-kind sofa bed.

Dual-tone upholstery for visual depth

Denite uses contrasting woven fabrics to create a subtle two-tone effect that brings dimension to smaller rooms. The accent striping and colour blocking keep the silhouette modern while remaining neutral enough to pair with different décor styles over time.

Multi-mode lounging versatility

Denite comfortably shifts from a chaise for coffee and laptop work to a daybed for power naps, then to a full bed at night. The design supports everything from solo downtime to long catch-up sessions with friends, ending in a restful sleep without having to move rooms.

Reconfigurable back cushions

Two loose back cushions can be propped up for structured support or laid flat to form a chaise-like daybed arrangement. This flexibility lets you tailor the feel—more upright for work or reading, more laid-back for napping, binge-watching, or lounging with friends.

Easy 2-step sofa-to-bed conversion

Transforming Denite from sofa to bed is a straightforward two-step process: pull out the under-seat drawer, then unfold the mattress section into place.

Dedicated mattress fabric in bed mode

In sofa mode, only the woven upholstery is visible; unfold it, and a softer, mattress-style fabric appears on the sleeping side, tuned for overnight comfort and breathability.

Space-smart solution for compact homes

Denite brings full sofa-and-bed functionality into a single footprint, helping you make the most of every square foot in smaller rooms. Ideal for studio apartments, guest rooms, or multi-purpose spaces, it lets one piece handle daytime seating and nighttime sleeping without cluttering the room.
