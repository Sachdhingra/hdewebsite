Before you buy

What to look for

Allow space behind

Most recliners need some room behind or in front to open fully. Check the space before you choose.

Manual or motorised

Manual recliners need no socket. Motorised ones open at a touch, which helps elderly parents. Ask us which models come in each.

Try the footrest

Sit back fully in the showroom and check that your legs are supported.
